# Test accounts

Every account below is created by `supabase/seed.sql` when you run `pnpm db:reset`. They exist only
in the **local** Supabase stack on your machine.

These are not secrets and are safe to keep in the repository: they are seeded into a throwaway local
database with a shared, obviously-fake password, alongside test trips and test money. Nothing in
PalaGo charges real money — see the payment section of [../README.md](../README.md).

**Never load this seed into a hosted project**, and never reuse this password for anything real.

## Notes worth knowing before you sign in

- **Roles are not self-service.** Sign-up always creates a `USER`; the `handle_new_user` trigger
  hardcodes it and ignores anything in the request metadata. Roles are applied by the seed, or
  administratively with the service role. You cannot register your way into `OPERATOR`.
- **Crew are no longer allowed in the passenger app.** As of Phase 8, `DRIVER` and `ASSISTANT` land
  in `(driver)` and the `(user)` gate refuses them. A crew account is a work account.
- **Two browser tabs share one session.** The app stores its session per origin, so signing in as
  the driver in one tab signs the passenger out of the other. To test two roles at once, use two
  browsers or a private window.
- **`pnpm db:reset` invalidates every session.** Any tab still holding a token for a deleted user
  will sign itself out on the next profile load — that is deliberate (`useProfile` signs out on
  `NOT_FOUND`), not a bug.
- The route guard is navigation only. What each account can actually *read* is enforced by RLS —
  see [security.md](security.md) and [auth.md](auth.md).

- **Two operators**: Cherry Bus (`CHERRY`) and RoRo Bus (`RORO`).
- **Six terminals**: Puerto Princesa `PPS`, El Nido `ELN`, Coron `CRN`, Roxas `RXS`,

  inventory is generated by a trigger.
- **Ten trips**, dated relative to `current_date` so the seed stays useful however long after it was
  written you reset — six Cherry (`CHERRY-<MMDD>-A` … `-F`) and four RoRo. Fares are centavos:
  ₱700.00 is `70000`.
- **Crew and one assignment**: Juan Santos and Maria Reyes are assigned to every Cherry trip.
  RoRo's crew (Pedro Ramos, Ana Lim) have **no linked accounts** on purpose — they exist so the
  isolation checks have someone to fail against.
- A couple of blocked seats, so the seat map renders something other than `AVAILABLE`.

## Signing in from a phone

`127.0.0.1` resolves to the phone itself, not your machine. Point
`EXPO_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL` and `EXPO_PUBLIC_WEB_PAYMENT_BASE_URL` at your machine's LAN address
first, or every sign-in will fail with a network error.

## Automated checks use these same accounts

`pnpm db:verify:all` signs in as real users rather than mocking, which is the only way to test RLS.
If you change an account's role or operator in the seed, the suites will notice.

```bash
pnpm db:verify:all
```
